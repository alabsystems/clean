# Inscribed angle theorem derivation
# Given: A, B, C, D on circle, C and D on same side of AB
# Goal: angle ACB = angle ADB

GIVEN on_circle(A, circle)
GIVEN on_circle(B, circle)
GIVEN on_circle(C, circle)
GIVEN on_circle(D, circle)
DERIVE congruent_angles(A, C, B, A, D, B) FROM inscribed_angle_theorem(A, B, C, D)
